Grievance Redressal

How parents, students and visitors can raise a concern, and how the school will handle it.

Last updated:

We want every concern to be heard and resolved fairly. Most issues are settled quickly by talking with the teacher concerned, so we encourage you to start there.

How to raise a concern

  1. Speak with the class teacher or the staff member directly involved.
  2. Write to the grievance officer if the matter is not resolved. Include the student’s name, class and section, a clear description of the concern, and any supporting details.
  3. Ask to meet the Principal if you are not satisfied with the outcome.

Child safety concerns

Any concern about a child’s safety or wellbeing is treated as urgent. Please raise it directly with the Principal. In an emergency, call 112. For child protection support, call CHILDLINE 1098.

Privacy concerns

Concerns about how we handle personal information are covered in our privacy policy.

If the matter is still not resolved

If you are not satisfied after following these steps, you may approach the relevant education authorities, such as the District Education Officer or the regional office.
