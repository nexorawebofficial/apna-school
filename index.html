Principal

From the Principal's Desk

Every child arrives with a question. Our work is to help them carry it further.

Dear parents and students, welcome. At Apna School we believe good schooling is measured as much by character as by marks. Our teachers know each child by name, notice what lights them up, and hold them to high standards with warmth.

Our classrooms are rigorous and our corridors are kind. We teach children to think clearly, speak confidently in more than one language, respect every culture that makes up India, and serve the community around them. We would be glad to welcome you on campus and show you what that looks like on an ordinary school day.

Why Apna School

Academic Excellence

Concept-first teaching, regular diagnostic checks and timely remedial support, so that no child reaches the board years with gaps.

Values & Character

Morning assembly, a house system, community service and everyday respect for every language, faith and family in our school.

Sports & Arts

Cricket, football, athletics, yoga and chess sit in the timetable alongside Indian classical and western music, dance, theatre and art.

Future-Ready Learning

Academics

One continuous journey in four stages.

Our curriculum follows the 5+3+3+4 structure of the National Education Policy 2020 and guidelines, so each stage builds on the last.

  1. 01

    Foundational

    Nursery, LKG, UKG, Classes I–II · Ages 3–8

    Play-based, activity-led learning that builds early literacy, numeracy and language, with the mother tongue and English side by side.

  2. 02

    Preparatory

    Classes III–V · Ages 8–11

    Reading for pleasure, hands-on mathematics, EVS projects and a first taste of a third language, with teachers who know every child well.

  3. 03

    Middle

    Classes VI–VIII · Ages 11–14

    Subject specialists, science and maths labs, coding, and experiential projects that connect classroom ideas to everyday life.

  4. 04

    Senior Secondary

    Classes IX–XII · Ages 14–18

    Board preparation with depth, subject choices in Class XI, and guidance on careers and higher studies. Streams offered: .
